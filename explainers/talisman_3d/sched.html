Feature groups (added after the first 40-iteration run)

That run showed no learning: waits are rare, one win or loss is a thin signal, and the policy had little to tell a good wait from a bad one. Four optional groups address that. All are off by default and turned on with --features; every extra engine search they need comes out of the move's node budget, so total nodes stay equal to stock's.
