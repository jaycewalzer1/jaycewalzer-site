I'm designing a model that expands what people find beautiful, rather than only matching what they already like. It pairs quality-diversity search, which keeps a spread of good options instead of a single best one, with a neural network that learns a person's taste.

Taste is hard to measure, so most of the work is in the data. With neuroaesthetics researchers I'm building art-specific ways to collect it: odd-one-out triplets (which of these three doesn't belong), free association, and mapping colors to emotions.

stack: quality-diversity search, PyTorch, human preference data
