At the Georgia Tech Research Institute I trained a 5.6-million-parameter AlphaZero-style chess agent in TensorFlow, using supervised tactics, Monte Carlo tree search and self-play. Then I tested whether searching across moves with different features helps it find answers its own policy ranks low, on 200 held-out positions with bootstrap confidence intervals. The full study is the [Concept discovery project](#concept-discovery).

stack: TensorFlow, MCTS, self-play
